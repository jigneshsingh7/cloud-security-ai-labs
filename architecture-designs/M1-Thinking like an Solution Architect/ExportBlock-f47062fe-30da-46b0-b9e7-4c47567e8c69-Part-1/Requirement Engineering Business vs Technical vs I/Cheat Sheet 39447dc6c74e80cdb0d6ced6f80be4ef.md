# Cheat Sheet

# Business Discovery (CEO / Product Owner / Business Team)

## 🎯 Objective

Understand **WHY** the solution exists.

---

# 1. Business Goal

### Questions

- Why are we building this solution?
- What business problem are we solving?
- Why now?
- What happens if we don't build it?
- What does success look like?
- How will success be measured?
- What is the expected business impact?

---

# 2. Users

### Questions

- Who will use the application?
- Internal users or external customers?
- Different user roles?
- Number of users today?
- Expected users in 3–5 years?
- Peak concurrent users?

---

# 3. Functional Requirements

### Questions

- What features are mandatory?
- Which features are optional?
- Any approval workflows?
- Any reporting requirements?
- Any document uploads?
- Any search capability?
- Any notifications?

---

# 4. Geography

### Questions

- Which countries will use the application?
- Any data residency requirements?
- Any latency expectations?
- Multi-language support?

---

# 5. Availability

### Questions

- Required uptime?
- Can the system go down?
- During which business hours?
- Any critical business events?

---

# 6. Growth

### Questions

- Current users?
- Growth expectations?
- Business expansion plans?
- Expected increase in transactions?

---

# 7. Budget

### Questions

- Budget limitations?
- Is cost optimization important?
- CAPEX or OPEX preference?

---

# 8. Timeline

### Questions

- Target launch date?
- MVP or full release?
- Any important deadlines?

---

# Technical Discovery (CTO / Technical Lead)

## 🎯 Objective

Understand **HOW** the business currently operates.

---

# 9. Existing Systems

### Questions

- Existing application?
- Existing database?
- Existing identity provider?
- Existing email platform?
- Existing monitoring?
- Existing cloud provider?

---

# 10. Integrations

### Questions

- Which systems must integrate?
- APIs available?
- SOAP or REST?
- Third-party vendors?
- Payment gateways?
- ERP?
- CRM?

---

# 11. Authentication

### Questions

- Existing SSO?
- Active Directory?
- Entra ID?
- MFA required?
- Password policy?

---

# 12. Security

### Questions

- Sensitive data?
- Compliance?
- Audit requirements?
- Encryption requirements?
- Data retention?

---

# 13. Performance

### Questions

- Expected response time?
- Maximum latency?
- Peak TPS?
- Batch jobs?

---

# 14. Scalability

### Questions

- Vertical or horizontal growth?
- Seasonal traffic?
- Black Friday events?
- Auto Scaling required?

---

# 15. Availability & DR

### Questions

- RTO?
- RPO?
- Multi-AZ?
- Multi-Region?
- Backup policy?

---

# 16. Operations

### Questions

- Who manages the platform?
- DevOps team?
- Monitoring?
- Incident response?
- 24×7 support?

---

# 17. Cost Optimization

### Questions

- Reserved Instances?
- Spot Instances?
- Storage optimization?
- Budget alerts?

---

# Architecture Discovery

Now the architect starts thinking.

## Constraints

Ask yourself:

- Budget?
- Timeline?
- Compliance?
- Skills?
- Legacy systems?

## Risks

Ask yourself:

- What can fail?
- Single Point of Failure?
- Security risks?
- Operational risks?

---

## Trade-offs

Ask yourself:

- Performance vs Cost?
- Security vs Usability?
- Simplicity vs Flexibility?
- Managed vs Self-managed?
